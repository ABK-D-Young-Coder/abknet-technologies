FAQ

Before you send it.

Can I report a problem with a tool?

Yes. Choose “Bug Report” or “Tool Feedback” and include the tool name, what you expected, and what happened.

Can I suggest a new tool?

Absolutely. Choose “Tool Feedback” and describe the problem you want the tool to solve.

Can I contact ABKNET about a partnership?

Yes. Select “Partnership” and include the organization, idea, and the best way to continue the conversation.

Will I receive a ticket ID?

Yes. Successful submissions receive a unique ABKNET ticket ID immediately from the backend.
